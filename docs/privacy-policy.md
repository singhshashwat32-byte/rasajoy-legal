# Indian Meal Planner (RasaJoy) Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 September 2026

Indian Meal Planner, also known as RasaJoy, is a meal-planning app developed and published by **Eternal Soft**. The Android application uses the package name `com.rasajoy.app`.

This policy explains what information the current version of the app stores, when information may leave your device, and the choices available to you.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores information needed to provide its meal-planning features, such as household composition, dietary preferences, dislikes, meal history, generated meal plans, grocery lists, reminder settings, custom dishes, meal-time preferences, and app preferences. This information is stored locally on your device by default.

The app does not require your precise location, contacts, microphone, camera, or access to your photos to provide its core features.

## Optional crash and stability reports

Crash reporting is optional and is off unless you choose to enable it in the app.

If you enable **Share crash reports**, Firebase Crashlytics may receive crash logs, stack traces, app version information, device information, technical diagnostics, and pseudonymous installation identifiers needed to diagnose app failures and stability problems.

The app does not intentionally attach your meal plans, backup contents, name, email address, or household data to crash reports.

You can change this choice at any time in **Settings > Privacy & diagnostics**. When crash-report sharing is disabled, the app keeps automatic Crashlytics collection disabled and does not intentionally send pending reports.

Firebase Crashlytics is provided by Google. Google may process diagnostic information according to its own privacy terms and policies.

## Optional Google Drive backup

Google Drive backup is optional and only runs when you choose to use the backup or restore feature.

When you authorize backup access, the app requests access to its app-specific Google Drive storage area. The app may receive the email address of the Google account you selected so it can identify the backup account in the app and store that account reference locally with backup status information.

Backup contents can include app data such as household settings, preferences, meal history, custom dishes, cook repertoire, onboarding state, selected cuisine packs, and meal-time preferences. Backup contents are encrypted using the app's backup encryption and the passphrase you provide before they are uploaded.

The app does not upload your local meal-planning data to Google Drive unless you explicitly use the backup feature. Google processes authentication and Drive storage according to Google's own privacy terms and policies.

## Advertising

The current release does not display advertisements and does not intentionally collect advertising identifiers. The release build removes the Android Advertising ID permission.

If advertising is introduced in a future release, this privacy policy and the Google Play disclosures will be updated before that release.

## Data sharing, sale, and retention

Eternal Soft does not sell your personal information.

Local app data remains on your device unless you explicitly use an optional Google Drive backup or enable optional crash reporting.

You can delete local app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Google Drive backups remain in your Google Drive app-specific storage until they are removed through the app's backup controls or through applicable Google account controls. Diagnostic information that you choose to send is processed by Firebase Crashlytics according to Google's applicable retention practices.

## Children's privacy

Indian Meal Planner (RasaJoy) is not directed specifically at children under 13, and Eternal Soft does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information handled by the app, including local storage controls and encryption for app-created Google Drive backups. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
