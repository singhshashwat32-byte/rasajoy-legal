# RasaJoy Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 31 July 2026

RasaJoy is a meal-planning app. This policy explains what information the current version of RasaJoy stores and how it is used.

## Information stored on your device

RasaJoy stores information needed to provide the app, such as your onboarding choices, household profile, dietary preferences, dislikes, meal history, generated plans, grocery lists, reminder settings, and app preferences. This information is stored locally on your device by default.

RasaJoy does not require your precise location, contacts, microphone, camera, or access to your photos to provide its core features.

## Optional crash reports

You can choose whether to share crash and stability diagnostics. If enabled, diagnostic services may receive crash reports, app version information, device information, and technical diagnostics needed to identify failures. RasaJoy does not intentionally include your meal plans, backup contents, name, or email in crash reports.

You can change this choice in the app's privacy and diagnostics settings.

## Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to use Google Drive backup, RasaJoy uses Google authentication and the app-specific Drive storage area to save and restore your backup. Backup data is protected using the backup passphrase and encryption features provided by the app. Google may process data according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Google Drive backup is optional. The app does not upload your local data unless you explicitly use the backup or restore feature.

## Advertising

The current release does not display advertisements and does not intentionally collect advertising identifiers. If advertising is introduced in a future release, this policy and the Play Console disclosures will be updated before that release.

## Data sharing and retention

RasaJoy does not sell personal information. Local app data remains on your device unless you explicitly use an optional cloud backup or enable optional crash reporting. You can delete local app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. You can delete cloud backups through the app's backup controls where available.

## Children's privacy

RasaJoy is not directed specifically at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information handled by the app. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
